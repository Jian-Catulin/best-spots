Jian's Top Spots in Makati and Manila

My personal guide to the best coffee, chill spots, and budget meals around the city.

Jian Jacob A. Catulin

I am a BTLED-ICT student at TUP. Between studying, coding, and managing my 20-hour work week, I am always moving around the city on my Honda Wave. Whether I need a break from a long shift or just a quiet place to unwind, these are my absolute favorite local spots to grab a budget-friendly bite, get some coffee, or just relax.

My Top Picks

Antipolo Church

Spiritual Site | Rizal

I highly recommend visiting the Antipolo Church. The Diocese of Antipolo is an ecclesiastical territory comprising the Province of Rizal and Marikina. It is considered to be one of the largest local Catholic churches in the Philippines, making it a truly significant spiritual and historical destination.

Tagaytay Day Tour

Nature & Food | Tagaytay

A Tagaytay day tour is a perfect getaway. It features cool mountain weather, panoramic views of Taal Lake and Volcano, and iconic viewpoints like People's Park in the Sky. After taking in the lush gardens, you can sit down to enjoy some hearty local food like bulalo.

Circuit Makati

Lifestyle Hub | Makati

Circuit Makati is a vibrant 21-hectare mixed-use riverfront development by Ayala Land, located on the former Santa Ana Race Track site along the Pasig River. Known as Makati's lifestyle and entertainment hub, it perfectly integrates residential towers, retail spaces, and open-air entertainment grounds to hang out in.
