Jian's Top Spots in Makati and Manila

My personal guide to the best coffee, chill spots, and budget meals around the city.

Jian Jacob A. Catulin

I am a BTLED-ICT student at TUP. Between studying, coding, and managing my 20-hour work week, I am always moving around the city on my Honda Wave. Whether I need a break from a long shift or just a quiet place to unwind, these are my absolute favorite local spots to grab a budget-friendly bite, get some coffee, or just relax.

My Recommendations

Nihon Cafe (Makati)

If you want a really good cup of coffee, Nihon Cafe in Makati is easily my top pick. It has a modern, luxurious aesthetic, but the vibe is still simple and completely laid-back. The ultimate combo here is pairing their coffee with a side of baked mac and cheese—it hits the spot perfectly. It is a fascinating little place and a great spot to chill.

Circuit Makati

When I just want to kill some time and get some fresh air, Circuit Makati is my go-to spot. It is a huge, open area where you can literally just sit back by the fountain and enjoy the view. It is perfect for unwinding, and since there is a mall right there, you can easily grab something to eat or check out some clothes while you are at it.

Suter Paresan (Sta. Ana)

You absolutely cannot beat Suter Paresan in Sta. Ana when you are hungry and on a tight budget. This is honestly the best beef mami and pares I have ever eaten. What really stands out is how clean the soup tastes. Even though pares is traditionally made with scrap meat, they prepare and season it so perfectly that it tastes like a premium meal. Mixing it all together is top-tier comfort food.
