Tagaytay Day Tour

A Tagaytay day tour is a perfect getaway from the heat of Metro Manila. Known for its cool mountain weather and stunning panoramic views of Taal Volcano, it is an excellent destination for riders looking for a scenic weekend trip. I highly recommend taking the scenic route and stopping for some bulalo to complete the experience.

Detail Information
Location Tagaytay City, Cavite
Best Time to Visit Early Morning (6:00 AM - 9:00 AM) to avoid traffic
Estimated Budget ₱1,000 - ₱1,500 (including gas and meals)
